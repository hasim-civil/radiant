Three ways Smart Attendance could look

Each screen below is the real dashboard, rendered by the production stylesheet at 360 px — the width of the most common Android phone. Pick one and the whole app switches to it.

Modern Light

White canvas, violet accent, soft shadows. Easiest to read outdoors and the lightest to render.

Premium Dark

Charcoal surfaces, violet glow instead of drop shadow. Comfortable at night, kinder to OLED battery.

Glassmorphism

Frosted panels over a gradient field. The most striking of the three, and the most demanding to paint.

Your choice is saved on this device and applied everywhere in the app. You can change it again from the theme control at the bottom of the sidebar.
